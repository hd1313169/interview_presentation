## Direction contract

THESIS: The deck is Ryan's website at slide scale, one quiet page with a single yellow marker pointing at what matters. It refuses the corporate navy template and decorative chrome.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, ink #141414, warm gray block #f0efec, thumb #efefef, marker #feffbf. Archivo with Noto Sans TC. 14px radii, 1px hairlines, no shadows. Section label is the site's square mark, label and hairline. Line-drawn Ryan illustration.

STORY: The reader sees the same person as the website, follows one highlighted idea per slide, and can follow the argument without a presenter.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Site hero composition. Section rule across the top, h1 at 92px left with 先做出來 marked, name beneath, illustration 640px right, URL and date at bottom left.
